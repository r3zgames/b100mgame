<h3 align="center">Neon flowers bloom through a machine world. A music-driven puzzle battle. Free public beta.</h3>

<p align="center"><a href="https://b100mgame.com"><b>▶ Play in your browser</b></a></p>

---

Nature is breaking into the machine. Swap glowing flowers, line up three or more of a kind, and chain the falls so your garden grows faster than the system can prune it. Every match plays a note, chains climb the scale, and the light around you pulses with the music. Big combos and chains send thorny seed pods to your opponent; crack them open into new blooms before your garden reaches the top.

**This beta:** you against the bot, five levels, on desktop browsers, phones and tablets (add it to your home screen to play full screen). Online versus and the 100-player Blackout royale come in later betas.

**Controls:** arrows or WASD to move, Z or Space to swap, hold X or Shift to raise the stack. On touch screens, tap a flower and drag it sideways; hold RAISE. Controllers work too.
